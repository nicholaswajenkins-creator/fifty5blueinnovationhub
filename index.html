01Why now

Platforms are losing the signal they optimise against — just as marketers face unprecedented growth pressure.

The last five years brought a 40% rise in the number of advertisers and a 60%+ rise in customer acquisition cost. The battle for attention has never been tougher, and proving incrementality has never mattered more.

01Regulatory

The model is training on a blur

Meta made Less Personalised Ads mandatory across the EEA in January 2026, using roughly 90% less data. Lookalikes, retargeting and conversion optimisation now train on a blended audience — an estimated 35–45% of active users.

02Structural

Walled gardens don't reconcile

Without person-level, cross-platform deduplication, frequency capping is useless and completion rates prove delivery, not human attention.

03Commercial

The buy side stopped trusting sellers

In August 2026 the ANA urged marketers to stop trusting retail media's own measurement. The buy side has said publicly it won't accept sellers marking their own homework.

Modelled signal is degrading. Observed truth is getting scarcer.

Which is exactly when the scarce thing — a representative, consented, single-source panel — becomes the most valuable asset in the chain.

03The test each product must pass

A framework for assessing product-market fit.

Four stages, from real problem to repeatable scale. Every product below is placed honestly against them — because knowing where something isn't yet is what earns the right to fund where it could go.

Stage 1
Is the problem real?

Customer‑problem fit

A real, urgent problem the audience already agrees is worth solving. The work is to listen, not pitch.

Stage 2
Can we solve it?

Problem‑solution fit

A prototype or pilot that resonates — high engagement and retention prove the solution lands.

Stage 3
Will anyone pay?

Business‑model fit

Free pilots become paid ones. Active paying users validate both the solution and the price.

Stage 4
Can it scale?

Product‑market fit

The market pulls: organic return, referral without prompting, growth with less effort.

Framework after Jeroen Coelen's four-stage model of product-market fit.

New product 01

AI Visibility Panel

The world is no longer searching. It's asking.
The gap
20+

Companies in AI-visibility measurement. The IAB said in August 2026 they use different methodologies that produce different answers for the same brand — prompting a new official framework.

0

Of them draw on empirical behaviour from real humans. Instead they generate synthetic prompts, simulating consumer behaviour and journeys.

16%

Of brands systematically track AI visibility today. CMOs name difficulty measuring impact their top challenge. Budgets are ready; the basis for spending them is not.

900M

Weekly active users on ChatGPT, and 2.5bn monthly on Google AI Overviews — now on almost half of searches and 14% of shopping queries.

The product

Consented capture of real assistant sessions from panel members, scoped to commercial categories.

Human-driven insight in compliance with the new IAB framework and its four P's — presence, prominence, portrayal, persuasion — all mapped to TGI for cross-channel audience activation.

Risk, threats & dependencies

Risk: consent design. People ask assistants about health, money and personal problems — this needs category-scoped capture and on-device redaction, built with legal from day one. Threat: Nielsen has just shipped a like-for-like product.

New product 02

Creator Verified Audience

The audience nobody has counted.
The gap
98%

Of surveyed buyers agree the industry needs a universal creator measurement standard — the role GRPs or CPMs play in traditional media (IAB, July 2026).

$44bn

Expected US creator ad spend in 2026. The category grew four times faster than media overall in 2025; 48% of buyers now call creators a "must buy".

67.3%

Of marketers still select TikTok creators primarily on follower count — a number reported by the seller, about an audience nobody has independently counted.

59%

Measure creator ROI only "somewhat confidently". 43% of senior buyers say stronger outcomes-based measurement would unlock more investment.

The product

Consented panel viewing identified by audio-matching ACR.

Outputs: verified real reach, demographic composition, duplication against a brand's existing paid social, and the category-buying rate of the audience actually watching. Mapped to TGI, the currency dataset — reporting which followers viewed and which didn't.

Risks, threats & dependencies

Dependency: platform permission, not methodology. Google's January 2026 cease-and-desist paused channel-level YouTube attribution. This version must run on own-panel observation only, be advertiser-funded, and launch under a JIC mandate.

The capability was proven — the permission was not.

New product 03

Signal Exchange

The auction optimises what you tell it to.
The gap
+14%

Median conversion-value lift Google reports from value-based bidding; Meta reports +32.9% conversions when the Conversions API is added. The platforms have already priced the uplift from a better signal.

Revenue

Is what almost every advertiser sends back as the value of a conversion — not margin, not repeat probability, not lifetime value. The algorithm then optimises toward the wrong definition of success.

~90%

Less data in Meta's Less Personalised Ads tier than the personalised one, on Meta's own filing. LPA went non-dismissible across the EEA in January 2026.

86%

Of commerce-media decision-makers rate stronger measurement a high or critical priority (Forrester). The appetite exists; the value signal the auction can act on does not.

The product

The advertiser or retail-media network sends the conversion event. Fifty5Blue overwrites nothing; the transaction record stays deterministic.

Panel truth reweights it into a value: LTV, predicted repeat-purchase probability, margin, and whether the buyer type is worth bidding more or less for. Returned as an enhanced, value-weighted signal through approved integrations.

Risk, threats & dependencies

The buyer is a growth or performance team, and we sell to insights — that's the gap, not the method. Also dependent on platform terms staying open to third-party value signals, and EEA consent scope covering panel-to-platform activation.

We calibrate what "valuable" means — the platform still bids.

New product 04

Competitor Adjusted Incrementality

The counterfactual nobody owns.
The gap
75%

Of retail-media advertisers name incrementality their single biggest measurement challenge, while only 15% describe themselves as effective at measuring it (Skai/Stratably, Feb 2026).

All

Clean rooms, walled gardens and retail-media networks measure inside their own loop. Competitor activity sits outside every one of them, by construction.

Guess

Is what most marketing-mix models use for competitive pressure — the least reliable variable in the model, and it drives the biggest budget decisions.

$1.35bn

Combined 2025 revenue of DoubleVerify ($748.3m, 33% EBITDA margin) and IAS (~$600m). Independent verification became a large business because the buy side stopped trusting seller numbers.

The product

An independent incrementality service where the control group is not merely unexposed people, but unexposed people adjusted for what every competitor was doing in that market that week.

Two value props, sold modularly or bundled: an audit layer over what platforms and retail-media networks self-report, and a competitive-pressure feed into clients' own mix models and geo-tests. The ANA asked for exactly this in August 2026.

Risk, threats & dependencies

Nielsen holds Ad Intel in the US, so our edge is international breadth — deeper outside the US since Vivvix went to MediaRadar in 2023 — plus the panel and the governance. The buyer is finance or procurement: if only insights signs, it prices as a report.

The one question no amount of first-party data can ever answer.
